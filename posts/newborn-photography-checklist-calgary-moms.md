Congratulations — you're growing a tiny human! Between the [prenatal appointments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prenatal_development), the nesting instinct kicking in, and the adorable tiny socks people keep gifting you, it's easy to let "book newborn photos" slide to the bottom of the to-do list.

Here's the truth most Calgary moms learn the hard way: **the best newborn photographers book up 2–3 months in advance**, and the ideal shooting window for those sleepy, curly, womb-like poses is just 5–12 days after birth. That's a razor-thin window to coordinate around a due date you can't actually predict.

That's why the smartest move is to work backward from baby's arrival — and start now. Consider this your complete, trimester-by-trimester checklist.

## Why Planning Newborn Photos During Pregnancy Is a Game-Changer

Newborn photography isn't like booking a haircut. Your photographer needs to reserve a floating spot around your due date, prep props, and hold time in their calendar for a session that could happen on any day within a two-week window.

When you plan during pregnancy, you get:

1. **First pick of dates** — popular Calgary studios fill their "due date bubbles" months ahead
2. **Time to build a real relationship** with your photographer (so the session feels relaxed, not rushed)
3. **Zero postpartum decision fatigue** — because trust us, at 3 a.m. with a newborn, you won't want to be comparing portfolios
4. **Better budgeting** — you can spread payments out instead of scrambling after delivery

### The Golden Rule: Book Between Weeks 20 and 32

Not too early (you might change your mind about style), not too late (you might lose your spot). The second trimester sweet spot gives you plenty of runway without over-committing.

## Your Trimester-by-Trimester Newborn Photography Checklist

Think of this like a baby registry — but for memories instead of gear.

### Second Trimester (Weeks 14–27): The Research Phase

1. **Define your style.** Do you love posed, wrapped, prop-heavy images? Or warm, natural lifestyle shots at home? Browse portfolios with intention — not just at 11 p.m. from your phone.
2. **Set a realistic budget.** Calgary newborn sessions typically range from a few hundred dollars for a mini session to four figures for a full custom experience with products.
3. **Shortlist 3 photographers** whose editing style makes your heart skip. Style is almost impossible to change later.
4. **Read the full contract** before falling in love. Look for rescheduling policies (because babies come when they want), illness policies, and what happens if baby arrives early.

*Pro tip for Calgary moms: If you're considering a studio-based [calgary newborn photographer](https://selda.ca/portfolio/newborn-photography/), ask whether the studio is heated to newborn-safe temperatures and whether props are sanitized between sessions. Alberta winters are no joke — you want a warm, cozy space, especially if you're driving in from Airdrie, Chestermere, or Cochrane.*

### Third Trimester (Weeks 28–40): The Preparation Phase

1. **Confirm your booking** and share your due date, hospital (Foothills, Rockyview, South Health Campus, or a birth centre), and any birth plan details that might affect timing.
2. **Plan wardrobe** — for baby, for you, and for siblings. Neutral, solid colors photograph beautifully; busy logos and neon tones do not.
3. **Schedule your own hair and makeup** for session day if it's in-studio. You'll be in some of these photos, mama.
4. **Prep siblings.** Toddlers do best with snacks, naps, and low expectations.
5. **Bag extras:** a plain onesie, a special swaddle or heirloom blanket, and any small meaningful props (grandma's knit bonnet, dad's hockey jersey — hey, it's Calgary).

### The First 48 Hours After Birth: The Execution Phase

1. **Send the announcement text** to your photographer. Most use a "due date bubble" system — one message is all it takes.
2. **Don't panic about the mess at home** if you've booked a lifestyle session. Photographers have seen it all.
3. **Feed baby on demand** before the session. A full belly is the secret to those sleepy, serene poses.

## Common Calgary-Specific Questions, Answered

### "What if my baby arrives early or late?"

Totally normal. A professional will have designed your booking around a *window*, not a date. Babies born at 36 weeks or in the NICU simply shift the plan.

### "What about winter babies?"

Calgary winters actually make for beautiful, cozy newborn photography — warm studio tones, knit textures, and soft window light. Just plan for travel and never rush on icy roads with a days-old baby.

### "Is it safe to pose a newborn like the photos I see on Instagram?"

Great question — [newborns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infant) are fragile, and safe posing is a trained skill. Composite editing (combining multiple images to "create" a pose) is how professionals keep baby secure while achieving those dreamy shots.
